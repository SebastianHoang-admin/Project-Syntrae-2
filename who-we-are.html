Who are we

We are building for people who want to match better with their crushes.

Syntrae is a crush-matching studio: a calm place to structure one sensitive crush choice, compare possible messages or timing, and see which response patterns keep returning.

What we believe

Crush tools should make judgment clearer, not colder.

Syntrae is designed around humility. It does not promise certainty. It helps people slow down, structure crush context, replay possible replies, and take responsibility for the action they choose.

Protected space

Sensitive context stays protected

People need a safe place to think through crush uncertainty. Syntrae treats crush context as protected input, not public performance.

Clearer paths

Relationship-signal clarity

Crush moments can feel noisy. Syntrae helps users organize the person, timing, message, and emotional risk, then see which response patterns keep returning.

Human choice

Human agency

The user still chooses. Syntrae can surface recurring replies, likely tradeoffs, and sensitive assumptions, but the result is shaped by the judgment, timing, and care a person brings.

Why founding members matter

We want the first version shaped by people who care about matching better with their crushes.

Founding members help us learn what people actually need: clearer crush inputs, better Syntrae Test views, stronger privacy expectations, and a product tone that feels supportive instead of cold.

How early feedback shapes the product

Early feedback informs the relationship scenarios, privacy expectations, Syntrae Test summaries, and support tone Syntrae prioritizes before launch.
