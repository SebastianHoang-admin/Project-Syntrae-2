The vision

Less guessing. More informed timing.

Syntrae is a planning layer for social decisions: describe the situation, state the outcome you hope for, and review statistically framed paths that may make the next move easier to choose.

Start with the moment

Use real context: the message, timing, constraints, and what a good outcome would look like.

Choose with care

Predictive science

Built to improve decision quality.

Syntrae uses statistical framing to help people prepare for important relationship moments. Your data is kept private, and the product is designed to improve certainty around high-stakes decisions without guaranteeing the outcome.

1

Data stays private

Your context, goals, and relationship details should stay protected. Syntrae is built around private preparation, not public exposure.

2

Decisions get clearer

Compare likely paths, tradeoffs, and confidence ranges before acting, so the next step is chosen with more evidence and less panic.

3

Outcomes are not guaranteed

Syntrae can improve certainty in high-stakes moments, but people and relationships remain complex. The product supports better judgment, not promises.
