The vision

Less guessing, more caring.

Syntrae is a planning layer for social decisions: Describe the situation, Explore the outcomes, and review statistically framed paths that may make the next move easier to choose.

Start with the moment

Use real context: the message, timing, constraints, and what a good outcome would look like.

Compare possible paths

See a few routes side by side with clear probability language instead of absolute promises.

Choose with care

Syntrae should encourage better judgment, not pressure or manipulation.

How Syntrae works

From context to clearer action.

Syntrae turns relationship context into private decision context, helping you understand patterns, compare paths, and act with more care.

Step 1

Build a persona

Create a respectful relationship model from the details that matter: communication style, shared history, timing, values, and the current situation.

Step 2

Interact with the persona

Explore messages, invites, and important conversations in a private space so you can compare possible responses before acting.

Step 3

Achieve insights

Review likely paths, tradeoffs, and confidence signals. Then choose the action that best supports the relationship you want to deepen.

Predictive science

Built to improve decision quality.

Syntrae uses statistical framing to help people prepare for important relationship moments. Your data is kept private, and the product is designed to improve certainty around high-stakes decisions while keeping agency where it belongs: with your actions.

1

Data stays private

Your context, goals, and relationship details should stay protected. Syntrae is built around private preparation, not public exposure.

2

Decisions get clearer

Compare likely paths, tradeoffs, and confidence ranges before acting, so the next step is chosen with more evidence and less panic.

3

Your action determines the outcome

Syntrae helps clarify the likely paths ahead, but the result is shaped by what you choose, how you communicate, and the care you bring into the moment.
