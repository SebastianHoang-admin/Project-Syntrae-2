Meeting the founder

A Mission Starts With A Human Story.

Hi, I’m Sebastian, the founder of Syntrae.

Like everyone, I have lived a life filled with highs and lows—moments of success and failure, happiness and heartbreak, choices I am proud of and mistakes I still carry. Yet, it often feels as though regret has occupied more space in my heart than joy.

There were moments when one different decision, a few kinder words, or a little more courage might have changed everything. In the silence that followed, I often wished for a chance to turn back, undo the pain, correct my mistakes, and finally make things right. But that chance never came, and the more tightly I held on to the past, the more incomplete I felt.

Eventually, I began to understand that while we cannot change yesterday, we can determine tomorrow. Our actions now shape the people we become. Time does not move backward because life is asking us to move forward, and I wish there were a better way to advance rather than learning from past mistakes, which implies that mistakes have already happened.

This is why I founded Syntrae: it is there to predict the outcomes of your actions and, hence, help you think through possible outcomes and turn the odds in your favor for a better decision.
