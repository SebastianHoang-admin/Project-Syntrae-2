Meeting the founder

A Mission Starts With A Relationship Story.

Hi, I’m Sebastian, the founder of Syntrae.

I built Syntrae from a familiar kind of uncertainty: liking someone, caring about the connection, and still not knowing what the next message should be. A few words can open the door to honesty, but the wrong timing or tone can make a good feeling suddenly feel pressured.

In romantic relationships, the hardest decisions are often small on the screen and large in the heart. Should you be direct or gentle? Should you ask now or wait? Should you make your feelings clear, or give the other person more room to respond?

I wanted a calmer way to think through those moments before sending the message, starting the conversation, or risking a misunderstanding. Not a tool that decides love for anyone, but one that helps people compare possible responses, notice repeated patterns, and choose with more care.

This is why I founded Syntrae: to help people improve romantic relationship decisions by replaying important choices across alternative outcomes, seeing which responses repeat, and making the next move with more clarity before the real action is taken.
