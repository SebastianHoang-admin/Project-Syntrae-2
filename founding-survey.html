Founding member survey

Tell us where romantic uncertainty gets difficult.

This short survey helps us understand real moments when people feel unsure what to say or do with someone they are romantically interested in.

Gentle feedback

Short, and Helpful.

Answer what you can. The goal is to learn how people handle uncertainty before speaking or acting, not to collect unnecessary personal details.

Your answers help shape product direction, language, and early use cases.

1. Tell me about the last time you felt uncertain about what to say or do with someone you were romantically interested in.

Use a general example. Please avoid names or identifying details.

2. What happened in that situation, and what made it feel uncertain or difficult?

Share the context, the decision point, and what made the next step hard to judge.

3. How often do situations like that happen for you?

A rough answer is enough.

4. What did you do at the time to handle the uncertainty?

Tell us what action you took, even if the action was waiting or doing nothing.

5. Who, if anyone, did you talk to before deciding what to do?

Keep this general: friend, sibling, therapist, group chat, no one, or another source.

6. What tools, apps, notes, advice, or habits did you use to help you think through the situation?

Include anything that shaped your decision process.

7. What made your current way of handling the situation helpful or unhelpful?

Share what worked, what did not, and where your process still felt weak.

8. What was the emotional, social, or practical cost of not knowing what to do?

Tell us what the uncertainty cost you in time, energy, confidence, connection, or outcome.

9. What would have counted as a successful outcome for you in that situation?

Describe what you wanted to happen, practically or emotionally.

10. Do you know anyone else who experiences this kind of uncertainty and might be willing to use Syntrae?

A yes, no, maybe, or general description is enough. Do not include anyone's private contact information.
