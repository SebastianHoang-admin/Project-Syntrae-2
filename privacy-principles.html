Privacy principles

Private context should stay private.

Syntrae's product direction starts from a clear trust obligation. The context people add should exist to improve their own decision rehearsal, not to expose, judge, or pressure anyone.

Product standards

Privacy, clarity, and responsibility should be designed together.

These are early principles, not final policy language. We want founding members to help us refine them before Syntrae launches.

Private preparation

Users should be able to think through sensitive moments in a space that respects the seriousness of personal context.

No mind-reading framing

Syntrae should describe patterns, probabilities, and scenarios without claiming access to another person's private thoughts.

Responsible action

Decision support should remind users that their tone, timing, and choices still determine what happens next.

Syntrae starts with the trust bar high.

If privacy and respectful language matter to you, your founding feedback can help refine that standard before launch.
